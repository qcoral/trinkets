Based off of this kit: https://www.sparkfun.com/sparkfun-weevileye-beginner-soldering-kit.html

Should glow when you cup your hand around it - super cute

List of parts:
- Green/yellow LED on underside (10mm)
- Coin cell holder
- NPN transistor
- 220 ohm resistor
- 10k (?) resistor (changed based on brightness)
- switch to kill the mechanism
